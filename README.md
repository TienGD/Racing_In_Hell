# 🔥 Racing in Hell

**Racing in Hell** is a thrilling high-speed survival game (endless runner). In this game, players take on the role of a hellish driver trapped in the depths of the underworld, desperately hitting the gas to escape. Your only mission is: **Dodge all deadly obstacles and survive for as long as possible.**

But beware, hell shows no mercy!

## 🎮 Key Features

* **Endless Gameplay:** Drive, swerve, and dodge obstacles until you crash. There is no finish line, only the limit of your reflexes.
* **Increasing Difficulty:** The longer you survive, the more frantic the pace becomes. The **Flame Guys** will continuously appear and jump at an ever-increasing speed, ready to crush you at any moment.
* **High Score System:** Automatically records and saves your highest score. Come back and break your own record!
